# Mieszko Office Operations

Internal multi-store operations platform for Mieszko.

## Module 1 — Supplier Orders

Initial suppliers:
- Polish Village Bread
- Starmalyn
- Polish Bakery
- P.J. Martin

Orders are entered in cases. Each product stores its case size, and total units are calculated from `cases × case size`.

This repository is designed to grow into a broader office operations platform with future modules for deliveries, claims, invoices, audits, reports and tasks.
